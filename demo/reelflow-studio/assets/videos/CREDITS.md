# Video credits

These three short clips stand in for the real video content that would sit in
the real ReelFlow Studio queue. They are real, freely-licensed footage
(6-second trims, re-encoded to VP9/WebM, no audio), not stock footage of any
real Digitalise Agency client, and not depicting any identifiable person's
face or voice (checked frame-by-frame before selecting each segment, since
using a real person's likeness or speech in a fabricated commercial context
would be a different, worse problem than using stock b-roll).
